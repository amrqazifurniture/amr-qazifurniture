Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

AMR.Qazi Furniture (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what may be collected when you visit this website or contact our team.

Information you provide

If you contact us by phone or WhatsApp, you may share your name, phone number, messages, furniture requirements or project details. We use this information only to respond to your enquiry and discuss the requested service.

Information collected automatically

Like many websites, basic technical information such as browser type, device information, pages viewed and approximate usage data may be processed by the website hosting or analytics services, if enabled.

Cookies and advertising

This website may use cookies and similar technologies to support site functionality and understand website traffic. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled in the future, its providers may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure advertisements. Advertising and cookie practices will be subject to the providers’ own policies and settings.

Third-party services

Links to WhatsApp, social networks or other third-party websites are provided for convenience. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. Please review them before sharing personal information.

How information is used

  • To reply to enquiries and customer requests.
  • To discuss furniture designs, quotations and orders.
  • To maintain and improve the website.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse.

Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by service providers that help operate the website or communication channels. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You can choose not to send personal information. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us using the details on our Contact Us page.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The latest version will be published on this page.
