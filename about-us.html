About Us

AMR.Qazi Furniture provides custom furniture and interior solutions in Karachi, Pakistan. Our website presents furniture collections, project inspiration and ways to contact our team.

Our work

We work across a range of furniture needs, including bedroom sets, kitchens, office furniture, bridal furniture, and made-to-order pieces. Customers can contact us to discuss their space, preferred design and requirements.

Experience and approach

Our business shares a heritage dating back to 1975. We aim to combine practical design, attention to detail and clear communication throughout each customer enquiry.

Custom projects

Every space and project can have different dimensions, materials, finishes and budgets. Contact our team to discuss the available options and request details for your project.

Visit or contact us

We are based in Liaquatabad, Karachi, Pakistan. For enquiries, please visit our Contact Us page.
