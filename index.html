Our Story & Craftsmanship

About AMR.Qazi Furniture

Built on craftsmanship, shaped by experience, and inspired by timeless design.

Since 1975, AMR.Qazi Furniture has been dedicated to creating furniture that brings comfort, functionality, and elegance into every space. What began with traditional carpentry has grown into a furniture business built on years of hands-on experience, creative design, and a commitment to quality.

Qazi M. Ashraf

Owner | Master Carpenter | Furniture Designer & Creator

With over five decades of experience in furniture craftsmanship, Qazi M. Ashraf brings together traditional woodworking skills and modern design concepts. His work reflects attention to detail, thoughtful designs, durable construction, and beautiful finishing.
