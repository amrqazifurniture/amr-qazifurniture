About The Business

Experience You Can
Build On.

1975 se Qazi M. Ashraf furniture craftsmanship ke shobay mein apne tajurbe aur hunar ke saath kaam kar rahe hain. 50+years ke is safar mein unhon ne quality woodwork, mazboot construction aur behtareen finishing ko hamesha apni pehchan banaye rakha hai.

AMR.Qazi Furniture mein har furniture piece customer ki zaroorat, pasand aur space ko madde-nazar rakh kar tayyar kiya jata hai. Traditional craftsmanship ko modern designs ke saath combine karte hue bedroom furniture se lekar kitchens, offices aur complete interior projects tak custom solutions provide kiye jate hain. Hamare liye furniture sirf ek product nahi, balkay aisa kaam hai jo quality, durability aur trust ke saath saalon tak yaad rahe.

50+ Years of practical furniture-making and business experience.
